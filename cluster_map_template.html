Chunk clusters

How to read this

Each dot is a chunk. Dots close together have similar content. Colours are the clusters found automatically.

Click a cluster on the left to highlight it, or click a dot to read the chunk and see its most similar chunks (lines on the map).

Scroll to zoom, drag to move.

Signs of a problem: a cluster made of one document only, a cluster of e-mails, or a dot whose nearest chunks are all from its own document.
